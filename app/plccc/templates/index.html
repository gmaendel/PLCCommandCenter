How to set up Telegram (5 minutes)
  1. In Telegram, open a chat with @BotFather, send /newbot and follow the prompts. It gives you a bot token that looks like 123456789:AAH….
  2. Paste the token below and tap Save token.
  3. Make a Telegram group for your alarms (or use one you have), add your new bot to it, then send any message in that group.
  4. Tap Find my chat and tap Add next to your group. (A private chat with the bot works too: just send the bot a message first.)
  5. Tap Send test. The test message should show up in Telegram within a few seconds.
